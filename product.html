FAQ

Only prices explicitly shown on the product listing are displayed. Customisation, quantity and delivery can change the final quote, so confirm on WhatsApp.

Can I request another colour?

For many products, yes. Availability depends on the current material/colour stock.

Can I order more than one?

Yes. Enter the quantity and send the enquiry; bulk or multiple-piece requirements can be discussed.

Can I send a reference image?

Yes. After WhatsApp opens, attach the reference image, sketch or screenshot in the chat.
